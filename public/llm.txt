# Wayzyy

Wayzyy is a modern, premium subscription-based homestay platform providing stays without the small print. It provides an alternative to traditional lodging booking platforms by charging hosts a simple flat-rate subscription instead of high transaction percentages, ensuring honest pricing for travelers and transparent, evidence-respecting policies for hosts.

## Core Principles

- **No Hidden Fees:** Pricing is honest and transparent.
- **Honest Reviews:** Review extortion is actively prevented. Warnings left for other hosts are not pullable.
- **Real Listings:** Listings are verified with time-stamped proof.
- **Hosts Heard:** Platform policies respect host submissions and direct evidence.
- **Receipts Respected:** Fair disputes resolving on objective facts.

## Website Map

- **Home Page (`/`):** Primary portal explaining the POV of both travelers and hosts, benefits of the flat subscription homestay system, testimonials, principles, and waitlist signups.
- **Privacy Policy (`/privacy`):** Explains how traveler and host data is securely processed, user rights, cookies, and regulatory compliance.
- **Payment & Refund Policy (`/payment-refund`):** Sets up standard billing practices, transaction rules, cancel limits, and guest/host payouts.
- **Host Terms of Service (`/host-terms`):** Detailed legal rules, account criteria, listing standards, and fee terms for hosts.
- **Guest Terms of Service (`/guest-terms`):** Detailed rules, behavioral guidelines, booking processes, and account guidelines for guests.
- **Property Share (`/property/:propertyId`):** Dynamic landing pages for users to preview shared property items and deep link directly into the Wayzyy native mobile application.
